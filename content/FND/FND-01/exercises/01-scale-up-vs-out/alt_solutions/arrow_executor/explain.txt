Columnar storage lets us read only the product_code and line_amount column chunks, so the
bytes read are far below the full CSV. The row-oriented CSV scan must read every byte. No
shuffle happens in this single-stage local aggregation, but a distributed group-by would
pay one. Amdahl's serial fraction here is the CSV parse plus the final combine, which caps
how much the pool can speed up. Reading fewer bytes beats adding workers.
