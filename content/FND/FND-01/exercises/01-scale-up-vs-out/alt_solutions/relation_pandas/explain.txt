The win is about bytes, not cores. Parquet is columnar, so projecting two columns reads two
small column chunks instead of the whole file; the CSV pool reads every byte of every row.
This single aggregation needs no shuffle, but a cluster group-by would, and that network
movement dominates. Amdahl's law caps the pool because the parse and final serial combine
can't be parallelized. Fewer bytes read is the reason one box can beat scale-out here.
